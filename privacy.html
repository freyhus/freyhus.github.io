Privacy Policy

Vids Made Easy · Effective October 23, 2026

Vids Made Easy is a personal tool that makes YouTube Shorts and publishes them to the owner's YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. Google's use of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and by using Vids Made Easy you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Information it accesses

  • Public YouTube data: search results and the public statistics of public videos (views, likes, upload dates) and channels (subscriber counts), used to find popular topics. This is not tied to any user.
  • Your YouTube account, only if you sign in with Google and grant permission: permission to upload videos to your channel, and read-only access to your channel's YouTube Analytics reports (for example views, watch time and subscribers gained per video).

It does not access your email, contacts or any other Google data.

How it uses, stores and shares information

  • It uploads only the videos you make with it, to your own channel.
  • It uses your analytics only to show you which videos perform best and to improve future videos.
  • Everything it collects, including the sign-in token Google issues, is stored only on the computer where you run Vids Made Easy. Nothing is sent to a server run by the developer.
  • Video performance numbers may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to suggest improvements for future videos. They are used only to produce those suggestions for you, never to train AI models.
  • Video scripts are sent to Microsoft's text-to-speech service to create the narration, and search terms are sent to Pixabay to find stock footage. Neither receives your Google account data.
  • Your information is never sold or used for advertising.

Vids Made Easy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and tracking

Vids Made Easy does not place cookies or similar tracking technology on your devices or browsers.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can remove Vids Made Easy's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. To delete everything it stored, delete its folder, including the sign-in token, from your computer.
